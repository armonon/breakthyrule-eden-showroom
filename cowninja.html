COWNinja

This is a prototype set in a working slaughterhouse. It shows blood, violence against people and animal slaughter imagery.

BRIEFING

  1. Get in. The staff gate on the east side is open. Every pen has a red lever; pull one to free its cows.
  2. Build the herd. Freed cows follow you. Ride one, send the rest with F, charge a gate with them to free the pen behind it.
  3. Break out. Open the main gate from the gatehouse lever, or break it with a big enough charge, and get the cows to the safe ground beyond.

Stay off the ground: the gantry over the farm lane runs from the staff gate to the hall roof; beams over the kill floor, the container yard, the dock awning, the silos, the barn loft and the water tower all connect high routes, and crawl holes (crouch with C) lead into the hall. Tall grass along the fences hides you when crouched.

Quiet or loud is your call: take guards down from behind and hide them in hay, or ride through them. The harder you hit people and police cars, the harder they hit back.
