COWNinja

This is a prototype set in a working slaughterhouse. It shows blood, violence against people and animal slaughter imagery.

You arrive on foot. Get inside, pull the red lever by the pen to free the herd, get on a cow and ride out through the north gate. Stay low and out of sight and take farmers down from behind, or do it loud: once you are riding, charge through anyone in the way and hit the truck square on its side to flip it.
