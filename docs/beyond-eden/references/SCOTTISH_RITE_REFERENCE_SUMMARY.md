# Scottish Rite Temple reference summary

## Historical basis

The selected Wilshire Scottish Rite Masonic Temple was built in **1960–1961**, with Millard Sheets associated with the monumental art program and Samuel E. Lunden as architect. The internal architecture atlas identifies the building as a later-period exception to the old-Los-Angeles collection because its nearly windowless mass, colossal relief zones, elevated entry, and ceremonial auditorium language are unusually well suited to Beyond Eden.

The atlas’s construction reading prioritizes:

- massive wall planes;
- shallow openings;
- relief-panel zones integrated with the building skin;
- a raised formal entrance;
- a major auditorium volume;
- underground and behind-stage circulation;
- human scale established by the 1961 dedication imagery.

## Translation into the proof slice

The proof does not attempt a literal replica. It translates the historical reading into an original modular kit:

- monumental limestone-like massing;
- abstract geometric relief bays;
- a nonliteral mosaic band;
- an elevated stair and landing;
- an original sealed ceremonial door with eight page sockets;
- a broad ritual plaza;
- south, west, and east approaches;
- low planters and walls that interrupt sightlines without creating a permanent fortress position.
