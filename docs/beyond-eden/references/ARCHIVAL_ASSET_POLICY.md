# Archival asset policy

The document `Beyond_Eden_Selected_Architecture_Atlas_Revised.docx` is included only so the local agent can understand the chosen world and the temple’s historical reference.

## Internal-only materials

- archival photographs;
- scanned plans or drawings;
- source-site screenshots;
- copyright-protected relief and mosaic artwork;
- third-party colorizations.

These must never be copied into `assets/`, exported GLBs, runtime textures, the Netlify upload, public screenshots, promotional artwork, or source-control commits intended for release.

## Safe derived work

The project may use historical material to derive:

- general massing;
- proportions;
- material categories;
- construction logic;
- approximate structural depth;
- original-versus-later alteration notes;
- original modular geometry that does not reproduce protected artwork.

## Source ledger rule

Every nontrivial historical design decision should be traceable in an internal source ledger. The ledger should record the archive, title/caption, date, item ID, URL, rights note, and the exact modeling decision it informed.
