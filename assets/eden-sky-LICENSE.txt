Eden sky lighting (assets/eden-sky.hdr)

Source: "Kloofendal 48d Partly Cloudy (Pure Sky)" by Greg Zaal, Poly Haven
https://polyhaven.com/a/kloofendal_48d_partly_cloudy_puresky
License: CC0 1.0 Universal (public domain)

Changes: 1k HDR box-downsampled to 512x256, sun clamped, written as flat RGBE by
tools/asset-pipeline/shrink-hdr.py. eden-lighting.js turns it so its sun sits over the scene's sun
and uses it for image-based lighting and reflections.
