Skeleton and skin weights: the MPFB2 (MakeHuman Plugin for Blender) game-engine rig data, CC0;
bones renamed to the showroom's names. Only the CC0 data is used, not MPFB2's GPL code.
Showroom adaptations: Blender-decimated low-poly body (~3.5k triangles, extra detail in hands)
and clothing (~2.6k), body and face morphs, clothing,
hair styling, materials and animation. No Grand Theft Auto assets used.
Realistic avatars: assets/character-human-full.js is the full-resolution hm08 body (CC0), skinned to the
same MPFB2 game-engine rig by transferring the low-poly body's weights (tools/build-human-full.py); the
MakeHuman skins, eyes, brows, lashes and hair fitted to it are CC0 (assets/mh/LICENSE.txt).
