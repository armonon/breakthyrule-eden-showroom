BREAK THY RULE

We’re seekers of knowledge and truth. Our mission is to make this site an archive of authentic culture, knowledge and information—a place to explore, learn and keep asking questions. We take great pride in our products and our Archive, and in the care we put into both.

01. THE BRAND

We’re a clothing brand from Los Angeles. We collaborate with artists on every drop and print everything in house, working with vintage finds, new garments and our own cut and sew.

02. READY NOW

We pick from garments already in our selection: vintage tees, crewnecks and some new pieces too. Having these garments on hand means we can get your order out sooner.

On hand. Out sooner.

03. MADE TO ORDER

Each garment is constructed in LA’s Fashion District, then printed in house and finished with its details and additions.

Allow 2–3 weeks for making. That time gives us room to work carefully, refine the details and give your piece the attention it needs.

04. YOUR NOTES

Have something in mind? Add a note to your order. We’ll consider your ideas and how we might incorporate them into the garment—a detail, a direction or something you’d like us to keep in mind.
